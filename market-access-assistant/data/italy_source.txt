AIFA Pricing and Reimbursement Pathway Note (Synthetic Example)
Product: Renalpix (fictional treatment for chronic kidney disease progression)
Indication: Slowing progression of chronic kidney disease (CKD stage 3-4) in adults with type 2
diabetes
Body: Agenzia Italiana del Farmaco (AIFA), Comitato Prezzi e Rimborso (CPR)

1. Background
Renalpix is an oral therapy intended to slow the progression of chronic kidney disease in adults
with type 2 diabetes. The manufacturer submitted a dossier requesting inclusion in the reimbursed
formulary (classe A) with a proposed price based on cost-effectiveness modelling against standard
of care.

2. Managed Entry Agreement
AIFA's Comitato Prezzi e Rimborso proposed a managed entry agreement (MEA) combining a financial
component and an outcome-based component:
- Financial component: a confidential discount off the list price, with a cost-sharing arrangement
  for the first three months of treatment in newly initiated patients.
- Outcome-based component: a payment-by-results scheme in which the manufacturer provides a
  rebate for patients who progress to end-stage renal disease or require dialysis within 24 months
  of starting treatment, based on registry-confirmed outcomes.
- Patients must be registered in the AIFA monitoring registry (Registro di Monitoraggio AIFA) so
  that treatment response and progression events can be tracked to support both components of the
  agreement.

3. Budget Impact Uncertainty
The committee noted that the budget impact estimate submitted by the manufacturer was uncertain
for the following reasons:
- The eligible patient population estimate relied on epidemiological assumptions extrapolated from
  international literature rather than Italian regional registry data, creating uncertainty about
  the true size of the treated population.
- Assumptions about the uptake rate among eligible patients in the first two years varied widely
  across the scenarios presented by the manufacturer, with no clear justification for the base
  case assumption chosen.
- The offset in dialysis-related costs assumed in the economic model depended on the same
  immature long-term outcome data being collected through the monitoring registry, creating
  circularity between the clinical uncertainty and the budget impact estimate.
- Regional variation in current standard-of-care costs across Italian regions was not fully
  reflected in the national budget impact model, which the committee considered a further source
  of uncertainty.

4. Registry and Real-World Evidence
AIFA required participation in the Registro di Monitoraggio AIFA as a condition of reimbursement,
with mandatory reporting of renal function decline, progression to dialysis, and adverse events.
This real-world data collection is intended to inform both the outcome-based rebate calculations
and a scheduled re-assessment of the budget impact model after 24 months of registry data.

5. Conclusion
The Comitato Prezzi e Rimborso approved conditional reimbursement under the combined managed entry
agreement described above, contingent on registry enrolment and a formal re-assessment once
sufficient real-world outcome and budget data have been collected.
